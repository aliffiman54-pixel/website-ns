Our story

Nightmare Syndicate started as a small MLBB team of seven, focused on ranked games and scrims. Commitments and limited time later took most of that squad in different directions.

In July 2026, with help from friends, NS launched as a full community for the millennial generation, and the response was strong. Today it runs eight main squads, works with sponsors and is preparing its own league.
